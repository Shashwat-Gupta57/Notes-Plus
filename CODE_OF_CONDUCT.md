# Code of Conduct

Notes+ follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) is welcome regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and take criticism of the work, never of the person.
- Stay on topic and keep discussions useful to others.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, or anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate here.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@Shashwat-Gupta57](https://github.com/Shashwat-Gupta57), through a
private channel: GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, or a private security advisory if it
involves the project's safety. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments, close threads, or block people who break this code, temporarily or for good.
