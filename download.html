Installing on Windows

01

Download

Get the .zip above. Everything it needs is inside: no Java, no installer.

02

Extract

Right-click it, Extract All, into a folder you'll keep, like Documents.

03

Run

Open Notes+.exe. If SmartScreen warns about a new app: More info, then Run anyway.

04

Sign in

Use the same account as on your phone and your notes come across by themselves.

!

Updating? Never uninstall first.

Install the new version over the old one. Uninstalling deletes the key that unlocks your vault, and its photos can't be recovered without a backup. Make a backup before any big change to your phone.
