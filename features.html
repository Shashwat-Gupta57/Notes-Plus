[ Map of the world ]

Three dimensions.
One app.

The overworld is your notepad. Two gestures open the worlds below it. Here is everything in each.

[ The overworld ]

Notes for everything

Tap New and pick what you're making. Every note becomes a card that previews what's inside, so the grid reads at a glance.

  • Text: plain words, instant to start
  • Lists: tick things off, see progress on the card
  • Journal: dated entries with your mood, photos and videos
  • Mind maps: drag ideas, fold branches, colour them, link them to other notes, tidy the whole map in one tap, share it as a picture
  • Sketches: draw with pens, markers and an eraser
  • Audio: record your voice, see the waveform
  • Pin, colour, search and filter by type; works fully offline
[ The sky ]

Your notes, on every phone

Accounts are optional. Sign in and your notes follow you; stay signed out and nothing ever leaves your phone.

  • Sign in with Google, or with email and a password
  • Pick a unique @username
  • Edits sync when you finish a note, and appear live on your other phone
  • Edited on two phones at once? Both versions are kept, never lost
  • Photos, videos and recordings go to your own Google Drive
  • Sign out keeping a copy, or delete the account and everything stored online
[ Dimension one: the vault ]

The vault nobody knows about

◀◀ On the notes list, swipe left and keep holding for 2 seconds.

A lock screen appears that wasn't there a second ago. Nothing in the notes app hints at it.

  • PIN, password or pattern
  • Wrong guesses lock you out for longer each time, up to an hour
  • Locks itself again when you leave
  • Screens can't be captured or seen in recent apps (you can allow screenshots)
  • Several vaults, each with its own lock: keep a harmless decoy
[ Deep in the vault ]

Your photos, encrypted

Every photo and video is encrypted with AES-256 the moment it enters, with a key locked in your phone's secure hardware.

  • Smooth grid for thousands of items: pinch to resize, drag to select
  • Full-screen viewer with zoom, swipe-to-close and video playback
  • Albums and favourites
  • Delete with undo; send items back to your gallery
[ The ender chest ]

Backups you can trust

A backup is only reported as saved after the app has read it back and checked every byte.

  • Encrypted backup file you can copy anywhere, and open on a new phone
  • Optional backup password, so the file opens without your vault lock
  • "Check a backup file" proves an old backup still restores
  • Google Drive backup that only uploads what changed, with restore points
  • Runs in the background with a progress notification; closing the app doesn't stop it
[ Dimension two: the chat ]

A messenger behind a wall

▶▶ On the notes list, swipe right and keep holding for 2 seconds.

A second lock, a second world. The chat and the vault never mention each other.

◷ waiting✓ sent✓✓ received✓✓ seen
  • Chat vaults with their own locks; each shows only its own conversations
  • Reach someone by @username plus a 4-character vault ID (blank = their main vault)
  • Photos and videos encrypted on your phone before upload; only the conversation holds the key
  • Shared chat backgrounds, private nicknames, profile photos per vault
  • Move a conversation to another vault without the other person noticing
  • Works across your phones: while one has a vault open, the others can only read
  • No notifications, ever: nothing announces that the chat exists
[ Your build ]

Make it yours

Colours, photos and backgrounds everywhere it matters.

  • Lock screen background: a colour, a photo or a video, with blur and dim
  • Backgrounds for every vault and every chat list
  • Note colours and pinning
  • Your Google photo, or your own, as your profile picture
  • Light and dark, following your phone
