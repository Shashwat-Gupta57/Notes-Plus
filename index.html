[ The overworld ]

Notes for everything

Tap New and pick what you're making. Every note becomes a card that previews what's inside, so the grid reads at a glance.

  • Text: plain words, with pictures between them
  • Lists: tick things off, see progress on the card
  • Journal: dated entries with your mood, photos and videos
  • Mind maps: drag ideas, fold branches, colour them, link them to other notes, tidy the whole map in one tap, share it as a picture
  • Sketches: draw with pens, markers and an eraser
  • Audio: record your voice, see the waveform
  • Pin, colour, search and filter by type; works fully offline
[ The sky ]

Your notes, on every device

Accounts are optional. Sign in and your notes follow you, phone to phone and phone to PC; stay signed out and nothing ever leaves your device.

  • Sign in with Google, or with email and a password
  • Pick a unique @username
  • Edits sync when you finish a note, and appear live on your other devices
  • Edited in two places at once? Both versions are kept, never lost
  • Photos, videos and recordings go to your own Google Drive
  • Sign out keeping a copy, or delete the account and everything stored online

New in 13

Notes+ 13: organised, shared, everywhere

Folders and an archive, links anyone can open, many windows on Windows, and the web catching up with the apps.

  • Folders and an archive. Put notes in folders, shown as tiles of their notes on the All page, and archive the ones you're done with. Both follow you to every device.
  • Share by link. Anyone with the link reads the note in their browser, no account needed, always your latest version. Group notes get invite links: open, sign in, and you're in.
  • Many windows on Windows. Pop a note out into its own window, or open as many as you like. Every window updates the moment anything changes, from any of them or from your phone.
  • Find and zoom. Ctrl+F finds words in any note; Ctrl+ and Ctrl- make them bigger or smaller, just on screen.
  • More of your style. Thirteen note colours, a theme of your own (light, dark, or high contrast), pictures between list items, and mind maps with more than one central idea.
  • Vault backups that only ever add. A new device never shrinks your Drive backup, and "Sync now" brings down what's missing before backing up what's new, on its own at every start if you like.
  • The web, more complete. Group notes, your profile photo, themes, and recordings that reach your other devices.

New in 12, still here:

  • Notes+ on the web. Every kind of note, right here: text with pictures, lists, journals, mind maps, sketches and recordings, in the Notes+ look. Saved in your browser; sign in and it syncs with your phone and PC. Open it.
  • Locked notes open everywhere. A note locked on your phone opens on the web with the same lock, and the other way round.
  • Backups you can hold. On Windows, Export saves any note as one .npnote file, colours and pictures included, for a USB stick; opening it brings the note back. Deleting a linked note sends its .txt to the Recycle Bin.
  • Locked notes. Lock any note with a PIN, password or pattern. It opens with that lock on every device, stays open for as long as you choose, and stays encrypted everywhere else until you decrypt it yourself.
  • The Editor, on Windows. Notes | Editor, one switch apart. Open any .txt, then Save to notes: edit it on your phone and the file on your PC changes too. Lock it, and the file is encrypted.
  • A real Windows installer. Start menu, desktop shortcut, your default text editor if you like, and Notes+ updates itself.
  • Your vault on Google Drive, from your PC too. One backup for both, opened with your main vault lock or a backup lock of your own.
  • Mind maps that never cut you off. Ideas wrap, take a sub-text, and new ones can go behind an idea as well as after it.
  • Calmer syncing. "Syncing…" when notes are on their way, no more conflict copies, and recordings from your PC play on your phone.
