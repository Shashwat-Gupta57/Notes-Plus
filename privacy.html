Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

Privacy Policy

The fine print, written in plain human words.

1. Without an account, nothing ever leaves your device

You can use every note type, canvas mode, and local encrypted vault without signing in. Your notes, sketches, recordings, and settings remain isolated on your local storage. The only network request made is checking for new releases.

2. What happens when you create an account

If you choose to sign in to sync across devices, your notes synchronize via Firebase Realtime Database with strict authentication rules. Only your authenticated UID can read or write your notes. Your encrypted vault contents remain locked with client-side derived AES-256 keys that never touch our servers.

3. Complete account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time in Account Settings. Doing so instantly purges your database records, username handle reservation, attachments, and authentication profile across all locations.

4. Zero third-party ad networks or tracking cookies

Notes+ does not run advertising trackers, Facebook SDKs, Google Analytics beacons, or commercial marketing cookies. Local telemetry is anonymous, aggregated, and respects user opt-out controls.
