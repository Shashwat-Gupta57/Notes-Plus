[ The fine print, in plain words ]

Privacy

Exactly what Notes+ keeps, where, and who can see it.

Without an account, nothing leaves your phone

You can use every note type and the vault without signing in. Then your notes, photos and settings stay on your device, and the only network request Notes+ makes is checking GitHub for a new version.

The vault is encrypted on your phone

Every photo and video in the vault is encrypted with AES-256 as it is imported. The key lives in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore) and never leaves it, so the files are useless if copied off the phone.

Backups are encrypted too, whether saved as a file or to your Google Drive. If you uninstall Notes+ without a backup, the vault is gone: nobody, including us, can recover it.

One honest limit: vault locks are stored on your phone, not hashed, so someone with full access to your unlocked, rooted phone could read them.

If you sign in: your account and notes

Accounts use Firebase Authentication (by Google). We receive your email address, and with Google sign-in your name and profile photo. Your @username is visible to other signed-in users who look it up.

Synced notes are stored in Firebase Realtime Database. Security rules make them readable only by your account. They are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored readable on Google's servers, where the app's owner could technically access them. Don't sync anything you wouldn't put in a cloud notes app.

Photos, videos and recordings in notes go to your own Google Drive, in a Notes-plus folder. Notes+ can only see files it created there.

The chat

Chat messages are stored in Firebase Realtime Database while they are in transit and for history: only the latest 100 messages of each conversation are kept online, readable only by its two members under the security rules. Like notes, message text is not end-to-end encrypted.

Photos and videos in chat are encrypted on your phone with a key made for that one file, then uploaded to the sender's Google Drive and shared by link. The key travels only inside the conversation, so the link on its own shows nothing.

Your profile photo (Google's, or one you choose) and any per-vault photo can be seen by people who chat with you. Chat vault locks are stored as salted hashes, on your phone and in your account.

Deleting your data

"Delete account" in the app removes your account, your synced notes, your username, your chat data online and the note attachments Notes+ put in your Drive. Photos you sent in chats stay in your Drive's Notes-plus/Shared folder until you delete them there, and messages you sent remain in the other person's copy of a conversation. Notes on your phone are kept until you delete them.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

No Google Analytics, no Crashlytics, no ad networks, no usage statistics. We don't know how you use Notes+, and we don't want to.

Permissions

  • Internet: sign-in, sync, chat, Google Drive and update checks.
  • Photos and videos: to import into the vault, and to show your recent photos in the chat picker. Only what you choose is used.
  • Microphone: only for audio notes, while you record.
  • Notifications: progress of long backups and restores.
  • Install apps: to install Notes+ updates you agree to.

On Windows

Notes+ for Windows follows the same rules. It keeps everything in your Windows user folder (%APPDATA%\Notes+), encrypts the vault the same way, and only talks to Firebase and your Google Drive if you sign in. Signing in with Google opens Google's own page in your browser.

Questions?

The source is on GitHub, including the database security rules. Open an issue there with anything you want to know.
