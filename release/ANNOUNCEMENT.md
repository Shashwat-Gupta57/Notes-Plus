## Notes+ for Windows 13.0: many windows, folders and links

Pairs with Notes+ 13 for Android and Notes+ on the web (notes.talken.in/app).

### New
- **Many windows.** Pop any note out into its own window (the button beside the colours), open
  another window on your notes (Ctrl+Shift+N), or start Notes+ again. Every window updates the
  moment anything changes, from any of them or from your phone. A file opened from Explorer while
  a note is open gets its own window.
- **Find and zoom in notes.** Ctrl+F finds words in text, lists, journals and mind maps; Ctrl+ and
  Ctrl- (Ctrl+0 to reset) change how big they look, just on screen.
- **Folders and an archive.** Folders show as tiles of their notes on the All page; archived notes
  wait under Archived. Both sync.
- **Share by link**, and invite links for group notes.
- **Thirteen note colours, a theme of your own** (light, dark, or high contrast), pictures between
  list items (paste or drop them in), smooth drag-to-reorder, and mind maps with more than one
  central idea.
- **Vault backups only ever add.** "Sync now" brings down what the backup has, then backs up what's
  new here; switch on "Sync when unlocked" to do it at the first unlock each time.
- Fixed: the last lines of long notes could be cut off, and Left/Right could get stuck in text with
  right-to-left characters. Decrypting a note now asks for its lock.

### Install
1. Download `Notes-Plus-Setup-13.0.exe` below and run it. Already on 12.0? Notes+ offers the update
   by itself. Your notes are kept (they live in `%APPDATA%\Notes+`).
2. If Windows SmartScreen says it doesn't recognise the app, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
