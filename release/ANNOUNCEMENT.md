## Notes+ for Windows 19.4: reminders, and fixes from 19.0

Pairs with Notes+ 19.4 for Android and Notes+ on the web (notes.talken.in/app).

### New in 19.4
- **Reminders**: a list item with a day now notifies you (a Windows notification while Notes+ is running) at its time, or at 9:00 on its day when it has none. Click it to open the list.
- **The date pane** keeps your changes as a draft until you press **Apply** (or Cancel), has a **Clear** button that's always visible, and an hour and minute wheel for any time: scroll it, drag it or use the arrow keys.
- **Cards** show the clock tag of the item due soonest, by time as well as by day.
- **Mind maps**: calmer idea colours, readable with white text in every colour (purple is purple again).
- **Fixes**: lists sometimes opened with huge blank gaps until zoomed; Enter in the `[[` note list now picks the highlighted title; "Save as template…" is now in every note's ⋮ menu, and New always shows where templates come from.

### Also from 19.0
Due dates and repeats on list items, search across every note, Ctrl+K, links between notes, templates, import and export, and mind maps where you Ctrl+drag to select ideas and act on them together.

### Install
1. Download `Notes-Plus-Setup-19.4.exe` below and run it. Already have Notes+? The app offers the update automatically. Your notes are kept intact.
2. If Windows SmartScreen says it doesn't recognise the app, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
