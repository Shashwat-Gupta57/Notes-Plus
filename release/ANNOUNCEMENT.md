## Notes+ for Windows and Linux 21.0

Pairs with Notes+ 21.0 for Android and Notes+ on the web (notes.talken.in/app).

### New in 21.0: Ultimate notes
- **A new kind of note** (New → Ultimate): words, checklists, mind maps, voice notes, days and pictures on one page, with no boxes around them.
- **Type `/`** on an empty line to put one in right there; the bar at the bottom does the same.
- **Anything inside anything:** the + beside a checklist item puts a mind map, checklist, voice note or picture under it; "Put inside this idea…" in a mind map gives an idea a page of its own (its badge opens it).
- **The handle** beside each element nests it, brings it out, moves it, adds below or removes it.
- **The formatting bar** now stays fixed and solid above the page instead of sliding over your words.
- **Update Notes+ on every device**: older versions open an Ultimate note as plain text.

### From 20.1
- **Lists:** each item's words now sit level with their tick box.

### From 20.0: resize and place pictures
- **Click a picture** in a note or journal to select it: drag the handles on its corners and sides to make it smaller or larger (a share of the page, in steps of 5%).
- **The bar on it**: S, M, L, Full and Auto (as it comes), left / centre / right, move up / down, view and remove. Right-click gives the same choices.
- **Drag the picture into your words** to move it there; it lands between paragraphs, never inside a sentence.
- Double-click views it; Delete removes the selected one; Esc lets go.
- The size and place are kept in the note (`![[IMG_….png|60%|center]]`), so a picture looks the same on Android, Windows, Linux and the web. **Update Notes+ on every device**: 19.9 and older show a resized picture as broken.

### Also from 19.9
Undo in text notes, the formatting bar that stays at the top, Sync now in a note's menu, and vault deletions that reach every device.

### Install
- **Windows**: download `Notes-Plus-Setup-21.0.exe` below and run it. Already have Notes+? The app offers the update automatically. If SmartScreen warns, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Linux**: `sudo apt install ./notes-plus_21.0_amd64.deb`, or make the AppImage executable and run it.
