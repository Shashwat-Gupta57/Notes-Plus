## Notes+ for Windows 19.0: the biggest update yet

Pairs with Notes+ 19.0 for Android and Notes+ on the web (notes.talken.in/app).

### New in 19.0
- **Due dates and repeats on list items**: the clock beside an item (or Ctrl+D) gives it a day, a time and a repeat. Overdue items show red, today's yellow; repeating items come back unticked on their next day. Cards carry a small clock tag.
- **Search every note**: every word, in any order, across all your notes (archived and templates too), with the matches highlighted and filters for kind, colour, folder, pictures and dates.
- **Ctrl+K** goes to any note or action.
- **Links between notes**: type `[[` to link a note, Ctrl+click to open it, and see what links back under "Linked from". Renames follow everywhere.
- **Templates**: save a note as a template and start new ones from it.
- **Import and export**: bring notes over from Google Keep, Apple Notes, Obsidian or any Markdown folder, and export everything as a zip of Markdown.
- **Mind maps**: hold Ctrl and drag to select ideas together, then move, colour, connect, disconnect, strip pictures from or delete them at once. Each idea now has its own colour, apart from its branch colour.
- **Restyled menus** in the Notes+ look, and zooming out a list now shrinks its rows too.

### Install
1. Download `Notes-Plus-Setup-19.0.exe` below and run it. Already have Notes+? The app offers the update automatically. Your notes are kept intact.
2. If Windows SmartScreen says it doesn't recognise the app, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
